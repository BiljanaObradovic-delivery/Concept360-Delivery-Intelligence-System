## The Problem With Standard DD in RWA

Financial DD on an RWA program captures the wrong signals.

Cap table, token economics, and market size do not tell you
whether the program will reach institutional-scale operations —
or stall six months after your capital is deployed.

The programs that stall do so at a specific operational layer.
Standard DD does not look there.

---

## What Operational DD Looks Like

A structured operational readiness assessment covers seven layers.
Each layer has gate criteria — conditions that must be met before the next layer starts.
Gaps at any layer are delivery risks, not technical risks.

| Layer | What DD should verify |
|---|---|
| L0 — Operating Model | Is there a board-approved governance structure? Are decision rights defined? |
| L1 — Regulatory & Legal | Is the CASP/VASP licence confirmed or on a credible timeline? Is the MLRO appointed? |
| L2 — Structure & Contracts | Are vendor MSAs signed? Is the SLA framework operational? |
| L3 — Technical Architecture | Is the custody architecture documented and auditable? Are oracle dependencies mapped? |
| L4 — Build & Audit | Has SC Audit #1 been completed? Is the DORA ICT third-party register in place? |
| L5 — Integration & Testing | Have the three mandatory sign-offs been obtained? Is the rollback plan documented? |
| L6 — Go-Live & Operations | Has the G-OPS gate been passed? Is the hypercare plan active? |
| L7 — Post-Launch, Steady State & Continuity | Is there an operational continuity structure in place post go-live? |

## The Signals Standard DD Misses

**Soft risks** do not appear in RAG reports. They cause the most delays:

- Stakeholder pseudo-consensus — alignment reported, ownership absent
- Regulatory fear culture — compliance treated as a blocker, not a delivery dependency
- Knowledge gap between blockchain and banking teams — decisions deferred, timelines slip
- Vendor opacity — SLA exists, escalation path does not

**Three delivery plans consistently started too late:**

- **Banking Strategy** — banking screening takes 8–20 weeks. Starting after the platform build means arriving at go-live without a bank account.
- **Exit & Wind-Down Strategy** — required in the Offering Memorandum before the first investor subscribes.
- **Key Person Succession Plan** — NCA approval for MLRO replacement takes 4–8 weeks. It must exist before departure.
