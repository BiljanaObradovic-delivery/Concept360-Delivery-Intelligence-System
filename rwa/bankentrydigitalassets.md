## The Entry Problem

A regulated institution entering digital assets is not building a new product.
It is adding a new regulatory surface, a new vendor ecosystem,
and a new set of operational dependencies — on top of an existing
compliance infrastructure that was not designed for them.

The failure pattern is not technological. It is sequencing.

---

## What the Sequencing Problem Looks Like

Banks and asset managers entering digital assets consistently encounter
the same operational gaps — in the same order:

**Governance is defined for the bank. Not for the new activity.**
The existing RACI does not map to tokenization workflows.
Decision rights that work for core banking break at the boundary
between traditional and digital asset operations.

**Compliance arrives late.**
MiCA obligations, CASP licensing requirements, and DORA ICT controls
are treated as legal workstreams. They are delivery dependencies.
When they arrive at L4 or L5, they become go-live blockers.

**The vendor ecosystem is underestimated.**
Custody architecture, oracle selection, smart contract audit,
KYC/AML pipeline, and banking integration are not procurement decisions.
Each has a lead time that affects the critical path
if not sequenced from L0.

**The operating model is not adapted.**
A wave-based core banking delivery model and a tokenization delivery model
require different governance structures, different RACI designs,
and different reporting architectures. Running both on the same model
creates operational drift.

---

## The Dual Delivery Challenge

Regulated institutions entering digital assets typically run two programs simultaneously:

| Context | Primary framework | Key dependencies |
|---|---|---|
| Core banking / existing operations | Wave-based delivery, BAU governance | Regulatory reporting, existing vendor SLAs |
| Digital asset / tokenization entry | L0–L7 sequential delivery | CASP licensing, custody architecture, MiCA compliance |

The integration point between the two is where most delays originate.
It is not a technical integration. It is a governance integration.

## What Structured Entry Gives You

- A delivery model that sequences MiCA/DORA compliance as a gate criteria, not an afterthought
- A governance structure that covers both the existing regulated environment and the new digital asset activity
